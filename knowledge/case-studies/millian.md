# Millian Travel
Sector: Outbound tourism

Growing travel company run on scattered files, outside agencies and manual pricing. Built one system: central knowledge base, package management, pricing calculator, automatic proposals, AI assistant, AI content, CEO BI dashboard.

## Numbers
- 6 systems, one database
- 4 of 4 pillars completed
- Live dashboard for the CEO
- Change a package once — price, proposal, assistant and dashboard all update
