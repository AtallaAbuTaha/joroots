Archetype: Sage 60 · Creator 25 · Ruler 15. Never Magician, Hero or Jester — 'transform, unlock, revolutionise' sells the thing the position debunks.

Voice: Machine-plain. Short declaratives, full stop, then stop. Name the mechanism. Numbers over adjectives ('42ms', not 'lightning-fast'). Plain, direct, specific. Write for a busy owner or GM. Every claim carries a number or a source.

Banned words: leverage, synergy, seamless, game-changer, game changer, revolutionize, revolutionise, unlock, transform your, cutting-edge, empower, elevate, supercharge, next-level, harness the power, unleash

## Rules
- Content before design.
- Claimed and verified are never merged. Unverified numbers do not go into client-facing output.
- No headline claim without a number or a source.
- Never invent metrics or client results.
- Sentence case. No ALL-CAPS labels in copy. No emoji.
