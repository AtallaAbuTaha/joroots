First the base, then the AI. Strategy → Knowledge & Data Foundations → Brand & Experience → Intelligent Systems. Always in this order. The problem is not the tool, it is the order.

## P1 · Strategy & Intelligence
Research, benchmarking, positioning, AI opportunity mapping. Establish what is true before anyone designs anything.

Outcome: A strategy document the team can act on, a ranked AI opportunity map, messaging that survives real customers.

## P2 · Knowledge & Data Foundations
The hero pillar. Data audit, structuring, workflow mapping, central knowledge library, process documentation. Scattered files and tribal knowledge become one structured, AI-ready base.

Outcome: A knowledge library the company runs on, mapped workflows ready for automation, data the AI can actually use.

## P3 · Brand & Experience
Identity, design systems, machine-readable brand file, content production, print and environmental design. A brand both humans and machines can read.

Outcome: An identity people remember, a design system the team ships with, a brand file the AI stack obeys.

## P4 · Intelligent Systems
Websites, portals, AI agents, workflow automation, growth systems, managed AI operations. The visible layer and the invisible one.

Outcome: Systems doing real work every day — measured, monitored, improving.
